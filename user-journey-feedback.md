# User Journey: Feedback Reward

## 1. Discovery
Student lands on the home page and sees the "Founding Student Program" section promising a free premium report.

## 2. Generation
Student proceeds to `/generate`, uploads their project, and sees the partial preview.

## 3. Incentive
At the preview screen, a prompt appears:
> "Love the report? Help us improve and get this full package for FREE."

## 4. Submission
Student clicks to `/feedback`, fills out the 5 fields with genuine details (e.g., "The SRS section was very helpful but the ER diagram needs better labels").

## 5. Confirmation
Student sees a "Success" message stating that the review is in progress.

## 6. Reward Notification
(Future) Student receives an email or sees a notification:
> "Your feedback was approved! 1 Free Credit added to your account."

## 7. Consumption
Student returns to their project, clicks "Unlock with Free Credit", and instantly downloads the DOCX and PDF.
